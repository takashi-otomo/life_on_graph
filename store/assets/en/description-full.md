# Life On Graph

A local-first health app that visualizes your sleep, steps and heart rate entirely on your device. Your health data is never sent externally.

## Features
- **Sleep stages**: deep / light / REM / awake on a hypnogram timeline
- **Steps trend**: by time of day and daily / weekly / monthly
- **Heart rate**: resting / max and heart rate during sleep
- **Integrated view**: sleep, steps and heart rate on a shared timeline
- **Calendar**: jump to any day / week / month; days with data are marked
- **Multilingual**: English, Japanese, French, German, Portuguese, Spanish
- **App lock**: protect with biometrics / PIN

## Privacy (local-first)
- Reads only sleep, steps and heart rate from Health Connect, **read-only** (data minimization)
- Stored on-device with **AES-256** encryption. **No external upload**, no cloud sync, no ads/analytics SDKs
- Delete all your data anytime from within the app

## Requirements
- Android 8.0+ with Health Connect
